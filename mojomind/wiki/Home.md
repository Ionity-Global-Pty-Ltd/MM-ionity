# 🌸 Welcome to the MojaMind (MM-ionity) Wiki

**MojaMind** is an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed for an 8-week Creative Resilience Intervention. It blends validated clinical instruments, adaptive micro-AI psychology, expressive digital art, on-device voice processing, 432Hz procedural soundscapes, and 2D/3D relaxation experiences.
