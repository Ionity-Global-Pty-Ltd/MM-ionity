Gather from the earth 🍃

Find something natural near you — a leaf, a stone, bark, a flower, soil, the sky. Take a photo or choose one. The studio reads the real colours out of it and gives you a living palette to create with. Nothing leaves your device.

Grow something alive 🌱

Choose a growth spirit, then tap the canvas to plant seeds. Your palette grows across the dark. Turn on Breathe & Grow to make it bloom with your breath — in for four, hold, out for six.
