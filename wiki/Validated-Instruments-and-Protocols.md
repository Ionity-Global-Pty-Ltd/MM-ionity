# 🧠 Validated Instruments & Clinical Protocols

MojaMind administers standardized clinical mental health and wellbeing assessment batteries, formatted in accordance with Stellenbosch University research protocols:

### Survey Batteries
1. **Mental Health (PHQ-9 & GAD-7)**:
   - Evaluates depressive symptoms and generalized anxiety.
   - Includes real-time risk mitigation triggers: if Question 9 indicates acute risk, safe routing to crisis support helplines (e.g., SADAG, Childline, Lifeline) is immediately presented.
2. **Lifestyle & Treatment Adherence (MARS-5 & Stigma-5)**:
   - Measures treatment adherence barriers and internal/social stigma.
3. **Personal Wellbeing (BRS & CAGE-AID)**:
   - Brief Resilience Scale assessment and substance/alcohol screening.
4. **App Usability Evaluation (Post-Survey)**:
   - Standardized usability metrics to quantify user satisfaction, accessibility, and cognitive ease.
