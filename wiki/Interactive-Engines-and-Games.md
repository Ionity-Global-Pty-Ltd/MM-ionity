### 1. 🌸 Moja Meadow: 2D Zen Garden (`js/game.js`)
* **Living Ecosystem**: A tranquil garden where participants tap the soil to plant flowers in SHOUT-IT-NOW brand colors, water growing buds, and attract fluttering butterflies.
* **Circadian Day/Night Cycles**: Automatically shifts between sunlit daylight, glowing sunset hues, and starry night skies with ambient fireflies.
* **Persistent Growth**: Flower growth formulas continue calculating in the background, greeting participants with blooming meadows when returning to the app.
* **Pentatonic Audio Chimes**: Gentle harmonic chimes generated dynamically on every interaction.

### 2. 🐝 Moja Bee 3D: Sunray Flight (`js/game3d.js`)
* **3D Projection Engine**: Custom Canvas 3D rendering pipeline built without bulky external 3D libraries (Three.js), maintaining lightweight performance.
* **Gameplay Mechanics**: Fly as a resilient bumblebee across sunflower meadows, gliding over winding rivers to collect radiant sunrays and pollen sparks.
* **Adaptive Flow State**: Micro-AI difficulty scaling adjusts speed and obstacle spacing based on user responsiveness to maintain a calming, non-stressful flow state.

### 3. 🫧 Moja Pop: Serenity Bubble Odyssey (`js/bubble.js`)
* **De-escalation Pacing**: Harmonic floating bubbles that prompt rhythmic, paced tapping to disperse negative thought clouds and somatic tension.
* **Visual Calming Feedback**: Soft particle bursts and gentle color blooms upon each pop.

## 🎧 Procedural Soundscape Generator (`js/soundscape.js`)

* **432Hz Harmonic Tuning**: Web Audio API synthesizers tuned to 432Hz natural resonance frequencies.
* **Multi-Layer Ambient Soundscapes**:
  * 🌧️ **Gentle Highveld Rain**: Procedural white-noise filtering with randomized raindrop impulse synthesis.
  * 🌊 **Atlantic Ocean Waves**: Low-frequency sinusoidal oscillation modeling ebb and flow surf rhythm.
  * 🌲 **Indigenous Forest Wind**: Dual-band pass filtered wind whooshes with randomized bird call harmonics.
  * 🌾 **Night Meadow Crickets**: High-frequency frequency-modulated oscillators creating calming evening ambiance.
