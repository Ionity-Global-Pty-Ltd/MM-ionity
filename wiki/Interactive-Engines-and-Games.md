# 🎮 Interactive Engines, Soundscapes & 3D Games

MojaMind includes three mindfulness and micro-pause game experiences:

### 1. 🌸 Moja Meadow (`js/game.js`)
- A 2D procedural Zen garden where participants plant SHOUT-colour flowers and cultivate serenity.
- Supports Day/Night cycles, gentle fireflies, and toggleable pentatonic chimes.
- Persists state in the encrypted vault.

### 2. 🐝 Moja Bee 3D: Sunray Flight (`js/game3d.js`)
- A real-time 3D flight engine built using HTML5 Canvas 3D projections with hardware acceleration.
- The player flies as a bumblebee collecting radiant sunrays and pollen across winding rivers and sunflower meadows.
- Integrated adaptive micro-AI difficulty scaling.

### 3. 🫧 Moja Pop: Serenity Bubble Odyssey (`js/bubble.js`)
- Harmonic bubble popping game designed for immediate somatic de-escalation and calm breathing pacing.
- Uses 432Hz procedural sound synthesis (`js/soundscape.js`).
