# 🎨 Creative Interventions & 8-Week Curriculum

## 📌 Creative Resilience Framework

MojaMind’s 8-week curriculum provides structured weekly expressive arts activities designed to cultivate internal agency, process lived experiences, and build emotional resilience through diverse creative modalities.

---

## 📅 8-Week Activity Syllabus

| Week | Activity Name | Core Theme & Therapeutic Goal |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Self-Portrait** | Exploring identity, self-compassion, and recognizing one's inner strengths. |
| **2** | **My Safe Space** | Identifying and visualizing real or imagined places of safety, calm, and protection. |
| **3** | **My Family** | Reflecting on chosen and biological family, connection, and social support systems. |
| **4** | **My Journey** | Mapping past milestones, overcoming obstacles, and recognizing personal growth. |
| **5** | **My Homestead** | Celebrating roots, culture, community grounding, and belonging. |
| **6** | **Vision Board** | Projecting positive future goals, dreams, and long-term aspirations. |
| **7** | **Letter to Myself** | Practicing self-forgiveness, radical kindness, and supportive inner dialogue. |
| **8** | **My Song of Strength** | Integrating resilience tools, celebrating completion, and expressing joy. |

## 🛠️ The 7 Modalities of Expression

Every weekly activity empowers participants to choose their preferred method of creative expression:

```
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │      7 MODALITIES OF EXPRESSION      │
                  └──────────────────┬──────────────────┘
       ┌───────────────┬─────────────┼─────────────┬───────────────┐
       ▼               ▼             ▼             ▼               ▼
1. 🎨 Physical   2. 🖌️ Digital  3. ✍️ Write   4. 🎤 Voice    5. 🌿 Nature
   Artwork          Canvas         It Out         Notes          Sculpture
                       │                           │
                       └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                     ▼
                              6. 📱 Digital Collage
                              7. 🥁 Beat Studio (Music)
```

### 1. 🎨 Physical Artwork
* Participants create tangible art using paper, pens, crayons, paint, or recycled materials.
* Photograph and upload into the app with automatic client-side image compression.
* Includes on-device **Moja Vision** color psychology analysis.

### 2. 🖌️ Digital Art Canvas (`js/draw.js`)
* Full in-app digital drawing studio supporting touch, stylus, and mouse.
* Tools: Smooth pencil, calligraphy ink, glowing neon brush, shape stamps, undo/redo, and direct image export.

### 3. ✍️ Write-It-Out Notepad
* Integrated multi-tab text reflection studio.
* Allows participants to write poems, letters, journal entries, or thoughts with continuous local autosave.

### 4. 🎤 Speak Up — Voice Recording (`js/voice.js`)
* Built-in Web Audio microphone recorder for spoken word, stories, and songs.
* Features real-time on-device speech-to-text transcription.

### 5. 🌿 Use Nature
* Encourages tactile engagement with natural elements (leaves, sand, stones, flowers).
* Submissions are photographed and documented with reflection notes.

### 6. 📱 Get Digital
* Photo storytelling, phone photography, and creative image collages.

### 7. 🥁 Make Music — Beat Studio (`js/beat.js`)
* 16-step rhythmic drum sequencer and synthesizer.
* Interactive 808 kick, snare, hi-hat, and melodic chime pads with tempo/BPM controls.
* Allows participants to compose personal "Songs of Strength" and save audio loops.

## 🌈 Moja Vision: Color Psychology Engine

When participants upload artwork, `js/draw.js` samples the dominant chromatic spectrum to provide gentle, non-diagnostic reflection insights:
* **Warm Spectrum (Golds, Oranges, Reds)**: Vitality, energy, passion, and courage.
* **Cool Spectrum (Blues, Cyans, Teals)**: Serenity, introspection, peace, and emotional clarity.
* **Earth Spectrum (Greens, Browns)**: Grounding, growth, nature, and stability.
* **Deep Spectrum (Purples, Violets)**: Imagination, spiritual depth, and transformation.
